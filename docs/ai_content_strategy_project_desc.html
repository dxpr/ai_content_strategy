AI Content Strategy turns your existing content into a strategic growth plan. The module analyses your site's pages, navigation, and sitemap, then generates prioritised content recommendations tailored to your specific domain and audience.

This module is part of the AI module ecosystem and included in DXPR CMS, a Drupal distribution pre-configured with AI tools and security best practices.

Transform your content gaps into growth opportunities. AI Content Strategy provides data-driven content recommendations organised by category and priority, helping content teams move from guesswork to strategic publishing.

What you can do

  • Generate content recommendations across configurable categories: content gaps, authority topics, expertise demonstrations, trust signals, and more
  • Review prioritised recommendation cards (high, medium, low) with specific, actionable content ideas
  • Edit recommendations inline with autosave; mark ideas as "Implemented" with optional links to published content
  • Export your entire content strategy to CSV for use in spreadsheets or project management tools
  • Manage everything from the command line with 22 Drush commands or through AI coding assistants

Working with recommendations

Each recommendation category on the dashboard contains one or more recommendation cards. Every card has a title, description, priority level, and a table of content ideas you can work through.

  • Inline editing: click any card title, description, or content idea text to edit it directly. Changes save automatically.
  • Track progress: tick the checkbox next to a content idea to mark it as implemented. The text gets a strikethrough and you can add a link to the published content.
  • Generate more ideas: click "Generate more AI ideas" at the bottom of any card to get additional content suggestions for that specific topic.
  • Add new cards: click "Generate more AI recommendations" at the bottom of any category section to add new recommendation cards to that category.
  • Delete cards or ideas: hover over a card title or content idea to reveal the delete button.
  • Regenerate all: click "Regenerate recommendations" at the top of the dashboard to refresh all categories at once.

How it works

AI Content Strategy reads your site's published content, navigation menus, and sitemap to build a comprehensive picture of your current coverage. It sends this context to an AI model through the Drupal AI module, which analyses your domain and identifies strategic opportunities across each recommendation category. All processing happens server-side through the AI provider API you configure.

Each category has its own AI instructions that you can customise. The module ships with default categories covering content gaps, authority topics, expertise demonstrations, trust signals, content series opportunities, and AI visibility optimisation.

Features

  • Configurable recommendation categories: create, edit, reorder, enable, or disable categories. Each has its own AI instructions and exports through Drupal's configuration management
  • Priority-based recommendations: every recommendation card is tagged high, medium, or low so you can focus on what matters most
  • Actionable content ideas: each recommendation includes specific content ideas you can check off, mark as implemented, or link to published content
  • Dynamic idea generation: request more ideas for any recommendation card or add new cards to any category
  • Inline editing with autosave: click any title, description, or idea to edit it directly on the dashboard
  • CSV export: download all recommendations and ideas for use in spreadsheets, project boards, or editorial calendars
  • Customisable system prompt: adjust the global AI instructions that shape how recommendations are generated
  • Site structure analysis: integrates with Menu UI and your sitemap to produce contextual, domain-aware recommendations

Installation

composer require drupal/ai_content_strategy
drush en ai_content_strategy

Post-installation

  1. Configure an AI provider at /admin/config/ai/providers (or install DXPR AI Provider for a free tier)
  2. Set permissions at /admin/people/permissions: "access ai content strategy" for viewing reports, "administer ai content strategy" for managing categories and settings
  3. Review recommendation categories at /admin/config/ai/content-strategy/categories
  4. Optionally customise the system prompt at /admin/config/ai/content-strategy/settings
  5. Generate your first recommendations at /admin/reports/ai/content-strategy

Customisation

Recommendation categories are configuration entities. You can:

  • Create new categories for specialised content strategies
  • Modify the AI instructions that guide analysis for each category
  • Enable or disable categories based on your current priorities
  • Drag and drop to reorder how categories appear on the dashboard
  • Export and import categories through Drupal's standard configuration management for multi-site deployments

AI Coding Assistant Integration

AI Content Strategy includes a built-in Agent Skills file that teaches AI coding assistants how to generate and manage content recommendations through natural language. Compatible with Claude Code, Codex CLI, Gemini CLI, GitHub Copilot, Cursor, and other tools supporting the standard.

After installing the module, run drush acs:setup-ai to enable AI assistant support. Your AI will then respond to natural language like:

  • "Analyse my site and generate content recommendations"
  • "Show me high-priority content gaps"
  • "Create a new recommendation category for seasonal content"
  • "Mark this idea as implemented and link it to /blog/new-post"
  • "Export all recommendations as JSON"

FAQ

Does AI Content Strategy require a paid AI subscription?

The module itself is free and open source under the GPL licence. You need an AI provider to generate recommendations. DXPR AI Provider offers a free tier that works out of the box; alternatively, bring your own API keys from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or any other provider supported by the Drupal AI module. The module makes one API call per generation request.

How does AI Content Strategy analyse my existing content?

The module reads your site's published content, navigation structure, and sitemap to build a picture of your current content coverage. It sends this context to a configured AI model through the Drupal AI module, which identifies gaps, authority topics, and trust signals relative to your domain. All processing happens server-side through the AI provider API you choose.

Can I customise the types of recommendations I receive?

Yes. Recommendation categories are configuration entities you can create, edit, reorder, or disable at the categories admin page. Each category has its own AI instructions that control how the model analyses your content. You can also modify the global system prompt to shape the overall tone and focus of recommendations. Categories export and import through Drupal's standard configuration management.

How do I track which recommendations I have acted on?

Each content idea includes an "Implemented" toggle that lets you mark it complete and optionally add a link to the published content. The dashboard shows implemented ideas with a visual indicator so you can see progress at a glance. You can also export all recommendations and their status to CSV for tracking in spreadsheets or project management tools.

Does AI Content Strategy work with multilingual sites?

The module generates recommendations based on all published content visible on your site, including translated pages. The AI model receives content in its original language, so recommendations accurately reflect your multilingual coverage. For sites with many languages, you may want to create separate recommendation categories per language to get more targeted suggestions.

Can I use AI Content Strategy from the command line?

Yes. The module ships with 22 Drush commands covering generation, reporting, card and idea management, category administration, CSV and YAML export, settings, and health checks. Run drush acs:setup-ai to also enable AI coding assistant support, letting you manage recommendations through natural language prompts in Claude Code, Codex CLI, Gemini CLI, or Cursor.
